Delistd is free and stays free: the tool, the broker list and the docs are MIT licensed and need nothing from us. This page is for people who would rather not set it up alone. Paying is never required, and nothing in the tool is held back for customers.

The service is offered by **Drumandbytes OÜ**, the company behind the project (Estonia). The tool itself remains a separate open-source project.

## Setup for one person

A fixed-scope session, done remotely with you at the keyboard, on your own computer:

- Create a dedicated mailbox for removal requests, so your main inbox is never involved.
- Install Delistd and fill in your profile.
- Send the first batch to the brokers that apply to where you live.
- Show you how to read broker replies and deal with the "needs action" items: confirmation links, ID checks and questions.
- Set up the unattended re-send, so brokers that re-list you are written to again.
- One follow-up message afterwards for anything that didn't make sense.

The scope is fixed. The time it takes is not: we go at your pace. If your setup turns out to need more than this, we stop and quote the extra before doing it.

**What you need ready:** a computer (macOS, Linux or Windows) and an email account you're happy to dedicate to removal requests (or we create a fresh one together).

### What stays with you

You keep every credential and all of your data. The mailbox password is entered by you on your own machine, and we have no remote access afterwards. We don't host your copy, store your details or see your replies. That is the point of doing it this way, and it is why we don't offer a hosted version.

### What we don't promise

Brokers decide how they answer. Delistd sends the requests, tracks the replies and keeps the evidence, but no one can guarantee a broker deletes your data. See [how Delistd compares with paid removal services](/compare/) for the trade-offs.

## Staying covered

Removal is not a one-off job. Brokers re-list people, new ones appear, and replies keep arriving long after the first batch has gone out. Most people don't mind the work so much as not knowing whether something needs them.

A support period takes that off your plate. We agree a length and a rhythm that suit you, for example a few months with a regular session each month. In those sessions we go through the replies together, deal with anything that needs a decision, and keep your settings and exclusions current, so you always know what has happened and what, if anything, is waiting for you. When the period ends you can renew it, change it, or carry on alone. The tool and your data are yours either way.

## For organisations

For teams that want staff, executives or clients removed from broker lists: onboarding several profiles, a review of how mailboxes and access are set up, help with the evidence export for your compliance people, broker-list work for your regions, and a support contact with agreed response times. The same rule applies: you run it, we advise.
