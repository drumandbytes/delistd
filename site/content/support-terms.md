## Rescheduling, cancelling and no-shows

Move a session free of charge by telling us at least 24 hours before it starts. With less notice, or if you don't turn up, the session may count as used. If we have to cancel or move a session, we offer you a new time or a full refund.

If, during a session, we find that setup cannot work with your mailbox or computer, we say so straight away and offer to try again with another mailbox at no extra charge. If it still cannot be done, we refund the part we did not deliver.
